[NocoDB](https://nocodb.com) is at the heart of the service: an online
relational database that works like a spreadsheet, with no programming.

- Tables, relations between tables, attachments (images, PDF), filtered
  views and data entry forms.
- An account for each team member, with their role: read, edit or
  administer. Partners outside the University can be invited.
- An API, to work with the data in R or Python, or to feed a showcase.
- An export of your data as CSV, at any time.

### EDDBs, for sensitive data

A second instance, reachable only from the University network, hosts
projects with sensitive data.

### Included

- Hosting on the University's servers.
- Backups, updates and security.
- Modelling your data, together with you.
- Migrating your existing data: spreadsheet, CSV, former database.
- Training your team.

### Not included

- Data entry, which remains your team's task.
