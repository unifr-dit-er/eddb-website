summary: A website to publish your data, fed directly by your database.

---

A showcase is a website developed by DIT-ER to publish all or part of
your data. It reads your database directly: what your team enters appears
online, with no export. You choose the published fields; the rest stays
private.

Three levels, on quotation:

- **Simple**: list, search, detailed record, standard design, one or two
  tables.
- **Intermediate**: advanced search, links between records, customised
  design, several tables.
- **Full**: interoperability (IIIF), maps, several languages, tailor-made
  design.

A showcase is served under eddb.unifr.ch or at its own address, such as
[datacant.unifr.ch](https://datacant.unifr.ch).

### Without a showcase: public access

A NocoDB database can also be opened for anyone to read. The data then
keeps the look of the NocoDB interface: a quick solution when a public
address is enough.

### Included

- Developing the showcase, at the chosen level.
- Its hosting and updates.
- Its address, under eddb.unifr.ch or at its own domain name.

### Not included

- The texts and images of the showcase, supplied by your team.
- The presentation page of your project on the University website: it is
  up to your department.
