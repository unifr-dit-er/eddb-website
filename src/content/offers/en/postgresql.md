For teams who need direct SQL access, from R, Python or a GIS such as
QGIS: a PostgreSQL database on a server dedicated to your project,
administered together with the DIT. Each project undergoes a security
review before it opens.

A PostgreSQL database can also be given a NocoDB data entry interface:
your team enters its data in NocoDB, and you analyse it with SQL.

### Included

- A server dedicated to your project, on the University's
  infrastructure.
- Backups, updates and security.
- Setting up the database and your team's accounts.

### Not included

- Your queries, scripts and analysis applications.
- Data entry, unless a NocoDB interface is connected.
