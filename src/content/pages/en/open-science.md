  - title: Data management plan
    text: >-

      plan as a storage and publication solution.
  - title: After the project
    text: >-
      A database does not disappear at the end of a grant or when your
      team leaves, EDDB keeps it. We also help you plan its long-term
      preservation.

Funders, starting with the SNSF, expect research data to be **FAIR**:
findable, accessible, interoperable and reusable. EDDB helps you get
there, together with the University's Open Science team.
