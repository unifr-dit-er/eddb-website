title: Open science
description: How EDDB helps you make your data FAIR, publish it and preserve it beyond your project.
blocks:
  - title: Our support
    text: >-
      We help you structure, document and describe your data from the
      start, so that it can be published, shared or deposited when the
      time comes. Together with you and the Open Science team, we look for
      the solution that suits your project.
  - title: Data management plan
    text: >-
      For an SNSF application, we help you make sure your data complies
      with your data management plan: EDDB is where you manage it during
      the project, and we help you prepare it for deposit in a recognised
      data repository.
  - title: After the project
    text: >-
      A database does not disappear at the end of a grant or when your
      team leaves, EDDB keeps it. We also help you plan its long-term
      preservation.
---

Funders, starting with the SNSF, expect research data to be **FAIR**:
findable, accessible, interoperable and reusable. EDDB helps you get
there, together with the University's
[Open Science team](https://www.unifr.ch/research/en/openscience/).
