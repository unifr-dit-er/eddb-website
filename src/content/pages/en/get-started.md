### Who can submit a request?

Teachers and researchers of the University of Fribourg, for a teaching or
research project. Your partners from other institutions can then be
invited to your database.

### How a project unfolds

1. **First contact.** Describe your project, your data and your timeline
   to us by email.
2. **Meeting.** We review your needs together and propose a solution:
   [NocoDB database](#nocodb), [PostgreSQL database](#postgresql),
   [showcase](#showcases).
3. **Modelling.** We design the structure of your database with you.
4. **Data migration.** We import your existing data.
5. **Access and training.** Each member of your team receives their
   access, and we train you to use the tool.
6. **Project life.** We remain available for your questions and for
   changes to your database.
7. **Publication and beyond.** Showcase, open science, preservation of
   your data beyond the project.

### Contact

Write to us at [eddb@unifr.ch](mailto:eddb@unifr.ch).
