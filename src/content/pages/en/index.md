EDDB (*Educational Databases*) is a service of DIT-ER at the University of
Fribourg. It supports teachers and researchers who need a database for
their project, from data modelling to publication.

## What we offer

### NocoDB databases

An online database with a data entry interface as simple as a spreadsheet:
tables, relations, attachments and permissions for each team member. A
secure instance, on the intranet, hosts sensitive data.

### PostgreSQL databases

For teams working with SQL, R, Python or GIS tools: a dedicated PostgreSQL
database, administered together with the DIT.

### Public showcases

A website to publish all or part of your data: search, detailed records,
several languages.

## Open science

EDDB helps you make your data FAIR, for instance by synchronising it with
the national DaSCH platform or by depositing it in OLOS.
